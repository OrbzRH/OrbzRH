What happens to a credit

Three outcomes. None of them is dumped.

Fig. 1
01 · HOLD

Credits arrive

Every hour, trading fees in OrbzVault become credits, shared pro-rata to your time-weighted balance. No staking, no claim.

02 · USE

Free AI

One API key reaches every major model. A $1.00 credit buys exactly $1.00 of usage at list price, no markup.

03 · SKIP

It burns

Credit left idle for 168 hours buys $ORBZ back and burns it on-chain, with a public receipt for every burn.
